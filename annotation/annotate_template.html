Which event is more recent?

A reading task for a Macquarie University COMP8240 student project. About 30–40 minutes; you can take a break and continue later.

What to do. You will see 100 pairs of short passages from Wikipedia. In every pair, one passage is about an older event and one about a newer event. All years have been replaced with YEAR.

Choose the passage you think describes the more recent event. If you have any hunch, go with it; use Can't tell only when you truly have no idea.

Clues people use: names of politicians or office holders, technology, how many times an event has already happened ("the 61st edition"), whether the text says something will happen, and so on.

Please: don't search the web or use AI tools. Read both passages once and go with your first impression. There are no trick questions.

Keyboard: 1 left · 2 right · 3 can't tell · Backspace previous pair. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
