Which event is more recent? (round 2)

A reading task for a Macquarie University COMP8240 student project. About 20 minutes; you can take a break and continue later.

What to do. You will see 40 pairs of short passages from Wikipedia. In every pair, one passage is about an older event and one about a newer event. All years have been replaced with YEAR.

Choose the passage you think describes the more recent event. If you have any hunch, go with it; use Can't tell only when you truly have no idea.

Clues people use: names of politicians or office holders, technology, how many times an event has already happened ("the 61st edition"), whether the text says something will happen, and so on.

Please: don't search the web or use AI tools. Please read both passages in full before answering; the buttons unlock after 10 seconds. Then give your best judgement. There are no trick questions.

Some passages describe disasters, crimes or conflicts. Choose Can't tell to skip any pair you would rather not judge, or stop at any time.

Keyboard: 1 left · 2 right · 3 can't tell · Backspace previous pair. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
